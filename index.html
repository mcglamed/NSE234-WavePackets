Calculation details

Wave number is related to wavelength by k = 2π/λ.

Δk = 2π/(λ₀ − Δλ/2) − 2π/(λ₀ + Δλ/2)

When Δλ is much smaller than λ₀:

Δk ≈ 2πΔλ/λ₀²

The quantity 1/Δk is a useful characteristic localization scale. Widths based on standard deviation, full width at half maximum, or the first zeros will differ by numerical factors.
